Overhead LV span, rules eskom/0.8.0 (every overhead value a placeholder until ESKOM-OHL is held).
A 72 m LV route from a transformer, ABC-3C-70 (mass 940 kg/km, 36 mm bundle, breaking load 20 kN, E 56 GPa on 70 mm²,
α 2.3e-5 /°C). Max span 40 m: poles at 0, 36 and 72 m, two spans of 36 m, one section between the terminal poles.
Ruling span S = √(2·36³ / 72) = 36 m.

w = 940 × 9.81 / 1000 = 9.2214 N/m. Everyday tension T1 = 15 % × 20 kN = 3000 N at 15 °C. EA = 3.92e+06 N.
State change T2²·(T2 − T1 + w1²S²EA/(24T1²) + α·EA·Δθ) = w2²S²EA/24, solved by Newton's method:
- Hot, 75 °C, no wind (Δθ = 60): T2 = 1714.42 N.
- Cold, −5 °C, wind 500 Pa on 36 mm: w2 = √(9.2214² + (500 × 0.036)²) = 20.2246 N/m, Δθ = −20: T2 = 5582.01 N.
Sag at the hot tension: w·L²/(8·T) = 9.2214 × 36² / (8 × 1714.42) = 0.8714 m.

Clearance on flat ground: a 7 m pole set 0.1 × 7 + 0.6 = 1.3 m deep, attached 0.3 m below its top, holds the conductor
at 5.4 m, leaving 5.4 − 0.8714 = 4.5286 m against the 5.5 m minimum: too low. A 9 m pole set 1.5 m deep holds it at
7.2 m, clearance 7.2 − 0.8714 = 6.3286 m.
