Hand calc, single balanced three-phase load at end of a 70 mm² ABC line.
ΔV% = √3 · I · L · (R·cosφ + X·sinφ) / V_nominal · 100
I = 100 A, L = 0.5 km, R = 0.443 Ω/km, X = 0.085 Ω/km, cosφ = 0.9, V = 400 V.
√3·100·0.5·(0.443·0.9 + 0.085·0.4359) = 86.603·(0.3987+0.03705) = 86.603·0.43575 = 37.737 V → 9.434 %.
