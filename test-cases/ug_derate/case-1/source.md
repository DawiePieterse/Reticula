Underground cable de-rating, rules eskom/0.8.0. The standard conditions are from 240-56030637 Rev 2 §3.9.1 o)
(1.2 K·m/W, 0.5 m, 25 °C); the factor tables are placeholders until SANS 10198-4 and the 240-56030637 annex are held.
One 100 m feeder of AL-4C-35 (in ground 108 A, 240-56030637 Table 7) from a mini-sub, laid at 1.75 K·m/W, 0.8 m, 30 °C.
Pipe share 5 % is under the 10 % threshold (§3.9.6 i)), so the in-ground rating applies. One cable in the trench.

- Soil: 1.75 lies between 1.5 (0.92) and 2.0 (0.82): 0.92 − 0.10 × 0.25 / 0.5 = 0.8700; at 1.2 K·m/W the factor is 1.0.
- Depth: 0.8 m gives 0.96; at 0.5 m 1.0.
- Ground temperature: 30 °C gives 0.96; at 25 °C 1.0.
- Grouping: one cable, 1.0.

I = 108 × 0.8700 × 0.96 × 0.96 × 1.0 = 86.59 A.
